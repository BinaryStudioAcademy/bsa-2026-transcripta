There are millions of pages of handwritten archives that nobody has digitised, because:

Typing by hand

3–5 minutes per page. 500 pages = a week of work.

Ordinary OCR

Cannot read handwriting at all.

Specialised HTR

You must label dozens of hours of material first, to train the model.

Transcripta offers a third way: zero preparation, and accuracy that grows while you work.

How it works

01 Upload a PDF of a 300-page parish register
02 Pick the preset “19th-century parish register” — or create your own
03 A minute later, see the first transcribed pages
04 Go through the pages: Enter — correct, or edit the text and Enter
05 Notice that from page 50 onwards corrections are far rarer Step 5 is exactly what the project exists for.
06 Export the result to CSV
~50 minutes of human time vs 20 hours typing by hand

Accuracy grows while you work.

Every word you confirm joins the document’s lexicon and feeds back into the prompt.

By page 50 the model already knows this document contains the surname “Ivanenko”, the village “Dykanka” and the phrase “born and baptised”. So it stops inventing “Ivanchenko”.
